How to surf

  1. Don't hold W on a ramp. Surfing is all strafe keys (A/D) and mouse.
  2. Hold the key toward the ramp. If the ramp is on your right, hold D. That stops you sliding off the bottom.
  3. Too high? Let go a bit. Holding the key too long climbs you over the top. Tap it to stay in the middle of the ramp.
  4. Follow the ramp with your mouse. On curves, turn smoothly so you keep looking along the ramp.
  5. Air strafe to steer. In the air, hold A + move mouse left, or D + move mouse right. Match key and mouse direction (that's your sync).
  6. Fell? You go back to the start of the stage. The clock keeps running, so try again!

Start with the First Wave tutorial map. It shows hints as you go.
